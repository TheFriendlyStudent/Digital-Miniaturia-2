## Background

The Al-Rishad family had deep connections to Amdota, having originally settled the city when it was founded. The Al-Rishad family had created generational wealth through selling goods to other early Amdota settlers. Most of the Al-Rishad family was wiped out by the Beinngrippe Plague. Only Mohammad Al-Rishad, Alexander Al-Rishad's direct ancestor managed to migrate to Dalek. When Mohammad Al-Rishad and his descendants married into Dalekosky familes, and the Al-Rishad family eventually became more Dalekosky than Rizakh. When Bouldaria-Excelor emerged from the Beinngrippe destruction, Mehmet Al-Rishad, Alexander's grandfather, attended the prestigious Amdota Institute of Technology. After graduating, Mehmet spent the remainder of his life in Amdota, working at the Griswold Aerolab and raising his family.

## Life & Career

### Early Life

Al-Rishad was born to Saadia Al-Rishad (nee Hamilton) and Terrence Al-Rishad in the Borough of Kashvada in Amdota in the time of the Union of Rishtonia. Terrence was a production supervisor at the Griswold Aerolab, providing the family with some relative stability to see through the Corpo-State Era. As a child, Alexander dreamed of following his father's footsteps and working at the Griswold Aerolab.

Saadia was a devout Hindu. She was well known in the family's neighborhood for her generosity and kindness. She had received a medical degree from Sankton University, and intended to pursue a career, but when she was pregnant with Alexander, she allegedly had a vision of Vishnu proclaiming that her son would be his incarnation.
