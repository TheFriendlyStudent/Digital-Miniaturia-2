## History

### The Founding

The Rizakhs were a group of homogenous nomadic tribes who inhabited their lands for centuries. The nomadic period led to a consolidation of societies in modern-day Titania. Though nomadism gradually declined, the Rizakhs still depended on hunting for food production. At the time, there was a very rudimentary political structure that existed at the time; warlords acted as leaders of their respective clans. These Rizakh clans were often at conflict with each other over trivial matters. 

After a long period of battling, one weaker Rizakh clan managed to master agriculture, which was discovered in the region, but not sufficient for most clans. Neighboring tribes noticed the abundance of food in this particular tribe and were prepared to fight each other for control of that particular tribe, believing them to be blessed by God. The warlord who was recognized as the leader of the agricultural tribe offered to provide their neighbors with surplus food, as well as to teach them how to master agriculture. The other warlords or the neighboring tribes agreed to the terms, and they formed a loose alliance. 

As more and more tribes joined the alliance and developed agriculture, the Rizakhs began to expand beyond their usual prowling grounds,  intermittently raiding the Stonish tribes to the south and the Luko-Scotch to the east. Despite some military successes, there was very little unity or solidarity among Rizakhs outside of the mere desire to survive. As disputes began to emerge, a drought crippled the Rizakhs’ food production, leading to famines and more internal strife as warlords tried to secure food for their clan. the hot-headed descendants of the warlords who founded the alliance began to fight amongst each other; the alliance began to fracture. Before a complete descent into anarchy could begin, the warlord Bogyvara I embarked on a campaign to conquer all of the tribes. Bogyvara convinced other tribes to join him and fight for him by promising to lead them to new lands with plenty of food.
### The Rizakh Empire

Under Bogyvara I, the Rizakhs became the most powerful empire in the world. Bogyvara I offered unity, and a different way forward for the Rizakhs. He discouraged conflict amongst Rizakhs, while inciting raids on neighboring kingdoms. The Rizakh Empire was revolutionary both militarily and societally. Bogyvara I raised a large army supported by newly domesticated elephant cavalry. 

The Rizakhs turned chaotic warfare into strategic combat. Bogyvara I launched a decades-long campaign that expanded the Rizakh Empire as far as the eastern bank of the Vostok River. The Rizakhs brutally raided any foreign settlements they encountered and ransacked entire villages for food and valuables. The acquired loot was used to fund cultural enrichment, education, infrastructure, and religious institutions. 

Following the sacking of Rockville, the Stonish city-states united to expel the Rizakhs from the Stonish homeland. The Rizakhs waged a devastating war on the Stonish, but one Stonish king managed to deliver a grand defeat. Large numbers of the western Rizakh army were annihilated and the survivors were forced to retreat.
### Excelaric Commonwealths (Excelor)

When the Rizakhs retreated and reorganized further east, the Excelarics arrived in lands abandoned by the Rizakhs and established several independent commonwealths that were often at odds with each other. The Excelarics were an Athiest sect facing persecution from the High-King of [[Scotchland]]. Convinced they could start a country that would outlast the Scotch Kingdom, Seymour Andover led a group of would-be settlers to land rumored to be empty. 

When the Excelarics founded new establishments, the Indigenous Vandal population attempted to destroy each one. The Vandals were the number one killer of the Excelaric settlers, ahead of diseases and natual causes. The early settlers were prone to extreme individualism and stubbornness, splitting themselves among several city-states, and unwilling to cooperate to implement an effective solution. Frustrated with the lack of meaningful progress in addressing the matter, Governor William Barrington of Addisonia commissioned Thomas Keene’s famous expedition that facilitated the first interactions Rizakhs and Excelarics. Keene successfully appealed to the Rizakh King Mahendra for aid in neutralizing the Vandal threat. 

Keene's heroics and Governor Barrington's leadership brought the Commonwealths together, as the individual territories began to cooperate and sign mutual agreements. Eventually, the Commonwealths to create Excelor, the first independent nation established for the Excelaric people. Governor Barrington became Prime Minister Barrington, while Thomas Keene served as the Excelaric representative to the Rizakhs. Prime Minister Barrington told King Mahendra that if the Rizakhs ever faced any threat, the Excelarics would stand alongside the Rizakhs to face it. When the Rizakhs faced another extreme drought, the Excelarics honored their word by donating grain and introducing new infrastructure to ensure such a devastating disaster could not occur again.
### The United Republic of Rizakhastan and Excelor (Titania)

The Excelor-Rizakh alliance became something greater when the two societies united to form the United Republic of Rizakhastan and Excelor. The new capital, Amdota, was built on the site of Keene and Prince Ramanath's first meeting. The Excelaric author Rudyard Putnam wrote a poem which popularized the legend of Titania, the purest being in all of existence, being defending by the Rizakhs and Excelarics standing on either side of her. Thus, it was during this era in which it was determined that the nation inhabited by Rizakhs and Excelarics would be known as Titania.

Considered to be the Second Golden Age in Rishtonian history, this era saw technological leaps and cultural flourish. 
### Tsardom of Bouldaria-Excelor

The Beinngrippe plague claimed the lives of the high-ranking members of the Titanian government and killed more than half of the Rizakh population. The eastern half of the nation descended into anarchy. The surviving provincial governments in the west intervened to establish the State of Excelor. Excelor managed to bring relative stability to the eastern half of the Rishtonian realm. However, their ability to industrialize was severely crippled. There was great anxiety about the potential of traditional Rishtonian enemies taking advantage of the situation. Meanwhile, the Stonosphere was more concerned with the Canadian Secession and the looming threat of war. 

As the two rivals faced their own crises, Stonish King Koltan Gormelliovich IV chose to extend an olive branch to the Rishtonians. Stonish missionaries and doctors were sent to treat and assist the population in eastern Rishtonia. Naturally, the Excelarics were suspicious of the URS’s true intentions, but the positive impact of Stonish aid was undeniable. The Stonish response to Beinngrippe led to unusually warm relations with the Stonish and its Stonosphere allies. As Excelor and the Stonosphere began to interact more, [[Bouldaria|Bouldarian]] industrialists discovered the untapped potential of Excelaric resources. 

The Bouldarian Tsar’s advisors begin to consider the idea of an alliance or union with Excelor. The URS saw a potential union between the two nations as a trump card for the Stonosphere as they competed with Canadia to industrialize. In Excelor, there was fierce debate over whether a union with Bouldaria was in the best interests of Rishtonians. The idea of being ruled by a monarch was controversial, but the Stonish proposed that there would be a Tsar and an elected Prime Minister who share power. In Zofia, the URS negotiated an agreement that created the Tsardom of Bouldaria-Excelor.

Bouldaria-Excelor’s success in the Great War brought Bouldarians and Rishtonians closer together. After the war’s end, it seemed that Bouldaria-Excelor would be a major player in the international arena. By this point, Rishtonia had fully recovered from Beinngrippe and had caught its industry up with the rest of the Stonosphere. However, the Bouldar-Rishtonian alliance proved to be weak without any war to reinforce it. 

Rishtonians were gradually becoming less satisfied with the Tsar and monarch rule in general. Rishtonians made aggressive attempts at reform that would have abolished the Tsar and much of the Bouldarian influence in government. Bouldarians opposed the reforms, and the following dispute led to the Bouldaria-Excelor Civil War. The Bouldarians were backed by the URS, who facilitated the agreement that created Bouldaria-Excelor. Ultimately, Excelor acknowledged the dissolution of Bouldaria-Excelor.
### Union of Rishtonia

Following the outcome of the Civil War, the Rishtonian politicians who pushed for abolishing the Tsar were deeply unpopular. Attempts at forming provisional and intermediary governments were failing. James Griswold, patriarch of the Griswold family, saw an opportunity to expand his family’s influence and power. Rishtonian tycoons rarely held government positions at the national level, but they often established connections with politicians, especially those in their home provinces. For centuries, tycoon families had held soft power in government in the form of receiving government grants to establish public services for provinces. 

Using his political and business connections, James Griswold successfully intervened in formal negotiations. James Griswold successfully argued that private entities must shoulder the burden of governance. He cited the province of Griswold as a successful example of his vision. With widespread support, the Union of Rishtonia was established with a extremely weak federal government, and very strong provincial governments which acted independently.

Extreme corporate greed came to determine policy agendas in the Union of Rishtonia. The President became a pawn used by families such as the Griswolds. All of the tycoon families would have some regional control, but no family had the same influence as the Griswolds. The tycoons often made drastic cuts and modifications to the national budget to increase profits. Most notably, they disbanded the national army, entrusting the provinces with national defense. In effect, the largest tycoon in each province was responsible for managing and commanding a provincial army. Some tycoons would have control over both a private army and a provincial army.

The provinces began intervening in conflicts independently on behalf of the UR. In several instances, provinces started fighting each other. The UR began causing internal and external chaos, leading to a war with Meehanistan and the Stonosphere that plunged the UR into debt. The individual provinces then proceeded to involve themselves in Chambria and the Meehani-Scotch War, prompting an ATAO invasion led by the URS and Eirikr. Due to the chaotic structure of the UR’s national defense, the UR could not stop the invasion. 

The provinces that were situated along the border could not field strong armies like Trinita or Griswold. The entire southern border was left completely undefended. As the ATAO made rapid advances, the entire nation was engulfed in chaos. Amdotite socialists attempted to establish an independent commune. President Bogin disappeared and was never seen again. The Meehanis, who began to distrust the ATAO, launched their own invasion from the east to prevent a full URS takeover. Ultimately, the UR was effectively partitioned by the URS and Meehanistan along the western border of Addisonia, but wasn't officially dissolved until after [[Alexander Al-Rishad|Alexander Al-Rishad's]] coup.
### The Partition

For over two decades, the Rishtonians were split into two nations: The ATAO-backed Excelaric Republic and Meehani-backed Rizakh People's Republic. The Rishtonian realm was the one of the many battlegrounds on which the Great Stonish Chess Match was contested. The Excelaric Republic was another rare instance of Stonish-Rishtonian cooperation, though in a more coercive context. The Rizakh People's Republic, under the leadership of Al-Rishad, broke several Rishtonian precedents, being a relatively authoritarian regime concerned with militarization and territorial conquest. 

Johann Moses
