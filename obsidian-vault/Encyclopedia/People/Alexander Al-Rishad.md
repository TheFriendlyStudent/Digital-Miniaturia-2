## Background

The Al-Rishad family had deep connections to Amdota, having originally settled the city when it was founded. The Al-Rishad family had created generational wealth through selling goods to other early Amdota settlers. Most of the Al-Rishad family was wiped out by the Beinngrippe Plague. Only Mohammad Al-Rishad, Alexander Al-Rishad's direct ancestor managed to migrate to Dalek. When Mohammad Al-Rishad and his descendants married into Dalekosky familes, and the Al-Rishad family eventually became more Dalekosky than Rizakh. When Bouldaria-Excelor emerged from the Beinngrippe destruction, Mehmet Al-Rishad, Alexander's grandfather, attended the prestigious Amdota Institute of Technology. After graduating, Mehmet spent the remainder of his life in Amdota, working at Rao Laboratories and raising his family.

## Life & Career

### Early Life

Al-Rishad was born to Saadia Al-Rishad (nee Hamilton) and Terrence Al-Rishad in the Borough of Kashvada in Amdota in the time of the Union of Rishtonia. Terrence was the Director of Telecom Research at the Rao Labs Campus in Amdota. As a child, Alexander dreamed of following his father's footsteps and working at Rao Labs.

Saadia was a devout Hindu. She was well known in their neighborhood for her generosity and kindness. She had received a medical degree from Sankton University, and intended to pursue a career, but when she was pregnant with Alexander, she allegedly had a vision of Vishnu proclaiming that her son would be his incarnation. Saadia often let Alexander tag along on her volunteer work, encouraging Alexander to get involved.

### Education

Al-Rishad was homeschooled by his mother until he was ten years old. He received his secondary education at the prestigious Canton Academy, where he was admitted on lottery. In his tenth and final year, he achieved the top rank of his class. His teachers identified him as a "uniquely bright" student, remarking on his success in advanced classes. Al-Rishad attended the Amdota Institute of Technology, his father's alma mater. It was during this time when Al-Rishad was first exposed to the ideology of socialism. In his third year, he was infamously expelled for insulting Mayor Alvin Strickland's son when they met at a debate competition.

### Military Service

After his expulsion, Al-Rishad's dreams of working at Rao Labs were effectively out of reach. He was unemployed and aimless. Desperate, and seeking a purpose, Al-Rishad enlisted in the Expeditionary Forces. In '16, he was deployed to the Eastern Scotch Front. Comrades described Al-Rishad as a "too charismatic to be a humble private".
